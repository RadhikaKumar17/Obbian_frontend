# Vehicle photos

Put one photo per vehicle here, named after the vehicle id (`creta.webp`,
`seltos.webp`, …), then register it in `lib/vehicle-images.ts`.

- Side or front three-quarter view, car centred, plain or transparent background.
- About 960×540 (16:9), WebP or PNG, under 150 KB.
- Use photos you have the right to publish.
